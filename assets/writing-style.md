# Writing style for application notes

- Short and specific to the company: the first line says why this company or
  this problem, in the posting's own terms.
- Plain sentences. No headings or labels inside the note ("Relevant work:",
  "Why me:", "Anti-bot check:" and the like are never written).
- Only full-time work and the VaartaAI project are cited. Internships are never
  mentioned in a note.
- Real numbers from the profile (35% fewer tokens, 20% better relevance, 10x
  candidate pools, 40,000+ users) rather than adjectives.
- No claims beyond the profile. A missing skill is left out, not hedged.
- Close with location and notice period in one line: "I'm in Bengaluru; my
  90-day notice is negotiable."
- At most 120 words.
