# Forward Deployed AI Engineer - Northwind Labs

**Location:** Bengaluru (hybrid) | **Experience:** 0-1 years | **Type:** Full-time, permanent

Northwind Labs builds an AI agent platform that operations teams use to automate
support workflows. You will deploy LLM-powered agents for customers, connect
them to customer APIs, and fix what breaks in production.

## What you'll do
- Build and ship agent workflows with Python and FastAPI.
- Integrate LLM APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic) and build RAG pipelines over customer docs.
- Write internal tools in React and TypeScript.

## Requirements
- 0-1 years of software engineering experience; strong projects count.
- Python, REST APIs, SQL. Experience with LLMs or agents.
- Nice to have: LangGraph, vector databases, Docker.

Salary: 14-20 LPA.
